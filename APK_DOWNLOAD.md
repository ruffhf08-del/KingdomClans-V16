## 🐛 **Troubleshooting**

### **Installation Fails**
- Clear Google Play Store cache
- Free up storage space (min 100MB)
- Restart phone
- Try again

### **Game Crashes**
- Force close app: Settings > Apps > Kingdom Clans > Force Stop
- Clear cache: Settings > Apps > Kingdom Clans > Clear Cache
- Reinstall game
- Restart phone

### **Performance Issues**
- Reduce graphics quality in settings
- Close background apps
- Free up RAM
- Update Android OS

### **Can't Download?**
- Use WiFi instead of mobile data
- Check download folder
- Try alternative download manager
- Check storage space
